Our Story

The journey behind handora.

handora was born from a simple but powerful idea: that the finest handmade treasures in India — the ones that carry the soul of tradition, the warmth of human hands, and the creativity of generations — deserve to reach every home that cherishes them.

Our founders, deeply moved by the craftsmanship they witnessed during travels across rural India, set out to bridge the gap between talented artisans and global customers. What began as a small effort to support a handful of weavers has grown into a thriving marketplace for authentic Indian handcrafts.

How It Started

It all began with a single question: why were some of the most exquisite, handmade products in India — textiles woven with care, wood carved by master craftsmen, pottery shaped by patient hands — confined to local markets, while the world outside never saw them? The answer was a broken supply chain, exploitative intermediaries, and a lack of access to wider markets.

handora was founded to change that. We started by visiting artisans in their workshops, listening to their stories, and understanding their needs. Our first collection featured just a handful of products from three artisan families. Today, we work with hundreds of craftspeople across India, each one a guardian of a living tradition.

Our Journey

The Idea

Discovering the extraordinary craftsmanship hidden in small towns and rural communities across India — and the injustice of its isolation.

Connecting Artisans

Building personal relationships with craftspeople, visiting their workshops, and understanding the unique value of each traditional craft form.

Growing the Community

Expanding our reach to bring authentic handmade products to a global audience, creating sustainable livelihoods for artisan families.

Looking Ahead

Preserving heritage by ensuring traditional skills are passed to the next generation, keeping India's craft legacy alive for centuries to come.

Why Handmade Matters

Mass-produced items fill our lives with convenience, but they often lack soul. A handmade piece is different. It carries the marks of human hands — the slight imperfections that make each item unique, the hours of focused attention, the creativity born of tradition and innovation.

When you choose handmade, you choose skill over speed, quality over quantity, and community over convenience. You support a way of life that values patience, mastery, and the transmission of knowledge across generations. You help preserve cultural heritage that would otherwise fade away.
