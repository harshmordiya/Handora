Shipping Information

🚚

We Deliver Across India

We partner with reliable courier services to ensure your handmade purchases reach you safely anywhere in India. International shipping is available to select countries during checkout.

⏰

Estimated Delivery Time

Major Cities: 2-4 business days
Tier II & III Cities: 4-6 business days
Remote Areas: 6-8 business days

💰

Shipping Charges

Orders above Rs. 999: Free shipping
Orders below Rs. 999: Rs. 79
Express Shipping: Rs. 149 (select locations)

Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation. During sale periods, holidays, or high-demand periods, please allow an additional 1-2 days for processing. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number once your order has been dispatched.

Tracking Information

After your order ships, a tracking number will be sent to your email and SMS. Click the tracking link in your email or visit the courier's website directly to check your package status. You can also track orders from your Handora account dashboard at any time.

Delayed Delivery

If your order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery time, we recommend first checking the tracking information online. If there is still no update, please contact our support team with your order number and tracking details. We will investigate with the courier partner and provide you with an updated delivery estimate.

Contact for Shipping Problems

Packaging damaged or delivery issues? Our support team is ready to help. Email us at support@handora.in with your order number, or use our Contact Us form. Include photos of any damaged packaging for faster resolution.
