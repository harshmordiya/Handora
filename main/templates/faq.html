Buyer FAQ

How do I place an order?

Browse our collection, add items to your cart, and click "Proceed to Checkout". Fill in your shipping details, choose a payment method, and confirm your order. You will receive an order confirmation email immediately.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI payments , Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery. All online payments are processed securely through our trusted payment gateway partners.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, we will send you a tracking number via email and SMS. Use the tracking link to monitor your package in real time. You can also track orders from your Handora account dashboard.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. Express shipping is available for select areas during checkout.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it, as long as it has not yet been shipped. After shipping, cancellations are not accepted, but you can return the item upon delivery for a full refund.

Can I contact Handora for help?

Yes! Visit our Contact Us page, email us at support@handora.in. Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM.
