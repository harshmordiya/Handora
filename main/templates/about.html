About Us

handora is a platform dedicated to bringing the finest handcrafted products from talented artisans across India directly to your doorstep. We believe in the power of handmade — every piece tells a story of tradition, skill, and love.

Our mission is to support local artisans by providing them a wider marketplace, while giving customers access to unique, authentic products that cannot be found in mass-produced stores.

From intricately woven textiles to beautifully carved wooden items, every product on handora is made with care and precision. When you buy from us, you are not just purchasing a product — you are preserving a craft and supporting a community.

Craftsmanship

Every item is crafted by skilled artisans using time-honored techniques passed down through generations.

Sustainability

We promote eco-friendly, sustainable products that reduce waste and support traditional methods.

Fair Trade

We ensure fair compensation for artisans, building a community of proud craftspeople.

Delivery

Secure packaging and reliable shipping partners ensure your purchases arrive safely.
